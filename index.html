What is Evolving Nexus

A deeper conversation.

We bring together insights from psychology, philosophy, science, spirituality and human development to explore two fundamental questions.

  • How is reality structured?
  • How do we find meaning within it?

Our work is grounded in the conviction that no single tradition, discipline or worldview contains the whole picture. Understanding ourselves and our world requires a deeper conversation, one capable of holding together inner and outer, reason and spirituality, ancient wisdom and contemporary knowledge, individuality and relationship, being and becoming.

Through courses, research, writing and dialogue, Evolving Nexus is developing an integrative approach to consciousness, meaning and human transformation.

Who it’s for

People whose work depends on how others think and change.

  • Coaches, consultants and facilitators You have outgrown the frameworks in general circulation and need something with more resolution to work from.
  • Leaders and managers You want to understand why teams, cultures and organizations develop the way they do rather than the way the plan said.
  • Contemplative practitioners You have a serious practice and no map that explains what keeps happening inside it.
  • Interfaith and religious practitioners You want a comparative, developmental lens on your own tradition without being asked to leave it.
  • Therapists, educators and researchers You work with helping people find meaning and want the structure underneath it.
  • The widely read You have found every account partial and are ready to do the harder reading rather than the next summary.

What’s our focus

Two paths of exploration.

Each path opens into its own body of work. Every program is taught live and remotely by Marie and Nish, in a cohort that moves together.

The Structure of Reality

How is reality structured?

Exploring the deeper patterns through which consciousness, cosmos and reality may be understood.

  1. The Diamond Paradigm: Fundamentals

    Learn to see the architecture underneath consciousness and cosmos, and use it on your own experience.

  2. The Diamond Paradigm: Advanced

    Take the paradigm further into developmental and nondual territory, and apply it to personal and collective transformation.

The Meaning of Life

How do we find meaning?

Exploring consciousness, human development, relationship, purpose and transformation.

  1. The Architecture of Human Consciousness

    Understand how you and the people around you actually develop, and why they see the world as they do.

  2. The Seven Principles

    See the structures every culture has used to make meaning, and recognize which ones you are living inside.

What you’ll walk away with

You stop collecting frameworks and start using one.

  1. One

    The pieces connect

    You have read widely and found that every account explains part of it and none explains the whole. The work begins by showing how those parts actually relate.

  2. Two

    The structure becomes visible

    Human development follows patterns. Once you can see them, people who baffled you become legible, including the version of yourself you used to be.

  3. Three

    Disagreement stops being noise

    You can tell the difference between someone who is mistaken and someone seeing clearly from a stage you have left, or not yet reached. Most argument is the second kind.

  4. Four

    It shows up in the work

    Coaches get a sharper diagnostic. Leaders understand why their teams move as they do. Practitioners find their practice finally has somewhere to stand.

Before you join

Frequently asked questions about Evolving Nexus.

Who is Evolving Nexus for?

Coaches, consultants and facilitators. Leaders and managers. Therapists, educators and researchers. People with a long contemplative practice. What they share is work that depends on understanding how people think, develop and make decisions. No background in philosophy or psychology is needed.

What will I learn in an Evolving Nexus program?

How human development works, and what that means for what a person can see, hear and act on. The Meaning of Life stream covers consciousness, development, relationship and purpose. The Structure of Reality stream covers the patterns beneath consciousness and cosmos. Both share one framework.

What is the Diamond Model?

Nish Dubashia’s own framework for the structure of reality, developed over four decades across philosophy, science and the contemplative traditions. It is the spine of the Structure of Reality programs at Evolving Nexus, and his own work rather than a synthesis of other people’s.

Do I need a background in philosophy or psychology?

No. The Architecture of Human Consciousness is the starting point and assumes no prior study. If you have already read widely, people in that position usually say the reading was never the hard part. The hard part was that no single book explains how the different accounts relate.

How long are the Evolving Nexus programs?

One live session a week, plus reading or practice in between. Courses run six or eight sessions depending on which one. They are taught as seminars rather than lectures, so the discussion carries much of the learning.

How are Evolving Nexus sessions run?

Live and remote, with both Marie Murtagh and Nish Dubashia present, in a cohort that moves through the material together. Cohorts run across the United States and Europe, and session times are set so people in both can attend live.

What happens when I join the Evolving Nexus mailing list?

You receive occasional reflections on consciousness, meaning and reality, and word of new courses, research and events as they are announced. One email to confirm, and nothing you did not ask for.
