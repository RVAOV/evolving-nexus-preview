What is Evolving Nexus

A deeper conversation.

We bring together insights from psychology, philosophy, science, spirituality and human development to explore two fundamental questions.

  • How do we find meaning?
  • How is reality structured?

Our work is grounded in the conviction that no single tradition, discipline or worldview contains the whole picture. Understanding ourselves and our world requires a deeper conversation, one capable of holding together inner and outer, reason and spirituality, ancient wisdom and contemporary knowledge, individuality and relationship, being and becoming.

Through courses, research, writing and dialogue, Evolving Nexus is developing an integrative approach to consciousness, meaning and human transformation.

Who it’s for

For those seeking a deeper understanding.

Evolving Nexus welcomes thoughtful, curious people who want to explore consciousness, meaning, human development and the nature of reality.

  • Independent thinkers and lifelong learners You have explored philosophy, science, psychology or spirituality and are drawn to deeper questions, fresh perspectives and the possibility of bringing diverse insights into a more coherent whole.
  • Coaches, consultants and facilitators Go deeper into consciousness, transformation, developmental frameworks, and bring a clearer lens to the work you do with others.
  • Leaders and managers You want to understand the deeper dynamics shaping individuals, teams and organizations, and explore more conscious, integrative approaches to leadership and change.
  • Contemplative practitioners You seek to connect the insights of meditation, spiritual practice and direct experience with a broader understanding of consciousness and human development.
  • Interfaith and religious practitioners You value your own tradition while remaining open to insights from others, seeking a deeper understanding of the relationships between diverse spiritual and religious perspectives.
  • Therapists, educators and researchers You help people find meaning, and this gives you the deeper structures of consciousness and development to work from.

What’s our focus

Two paths of exploration.

Each path opens into its own body of work. Every program is taught live and remotely by Marie and Nish, in a cohort that moves together.

The Meaning of Life

How do we find meaning?

Exploring consciousness, human development, relationship, purpose and transformation.

  1. The Architecture of Human Consciousness

    Explore how human consciousness develops, how different perspectives shape our experience of reality, and how expanding awareness can deepen our understanding of ourselves, others and the world we inhabit.

    Drawing on developmental psychology, philosophy and contemplative wisdom, this program offers a richer understanding of human identity, meaning and transformation.

  2. The Seven Principles

    Explore seven fundamental dimensions of human existence: Spirit, Self, Suffering, Hope, Knowledge, Salvation and Love.

    Bringing philosophy, psychology and the world’s spiritual traditions into dialogue, this program offers an integrative framework for exploring life’s deepest questions and discovering new possibilities for meaning, understanding and transformation.

The Structure of Reality

How is reality structured?

Exploring the deeper patterns through which consciousness, cosmos and reality may be understood.

  1. The Diamond Paradigm: Fundamentals

    Discover the foundations of the Diamond Paradigm, an original integrative framework for understanding the deeper structures and relationships underlying reality.

    Explore how apparently opposing perspectives can reveal complementary dimensions of a greater whole, and how this understanding can illuminate consciousness, existence and everyday life.

  2. The Diamond Paradigm: Advanced

    Deepen your exploration of the Diamond Paradigm through its advanced philosophical, developmental and nondual dimensions.

    Examine the deeper relationships between consciousness, reality and transformation, integrating multiple perspectives while exploring the dynamic interplay of unity, diversity and the evolving whole.

Before you join

Frequently asked questions about Evolving Nexus.

Who is Evolving Nexus for?

Evolving Nexus welcomes coaches, consultants, facilitators, leaders, therapists, educators, researchers, contemplative practitioners and independent thinkers. What unites them is a curiosity about consciousness, human development, meaning and reality. No prior study is required.

What will I learn in an Evolving Nexus program?

You will explore how consciousness develops, how perspectives shape understanding, and how deeper insight enriches life. The Meaning of Life stream covers consciousness, development, relationship and purpose, while the Structure of Reality stream investigates the patterns underlying existence.

What is the Diamond Model?

It is Nish Dubashia’s original framework for understanding the structure of reality, refined over four decades across philosophy, science and the contemplative traditions, and the foundation of the Structure of Reality programs. It is his own work rather than a synthesis of other people’s ideas.

Do I need a background in philosophy or psychology?

No. Our introductory programs are accessible without prior academic study. Whether you are new to these questions or have explored them for many years, Evolving Nexus offers opportunities to deepen your understanding, connect different perspectives and participate in meaningful dialogue.

How long are the Evolving Nexus programs?

One live session a week, plus reading or practice in between. Courses run six or eight sessions depending on which one. They are taught as seminars rather than lectures, so the discussion carries much of the learning.

How are Evolving Nexus sessions run?

Live and remote, with both Marie Murtagh and Nish Dubashia present, in a cohort that moves through the material together. Cohorts run across the United States and Europe, and session times are set so people in both can attend live.

What happens when I join the Evolving Nexus mailing list?

You receive occasional reflections on consciousness, meaning and reality, and word of new courses, research and events as they are announced. One email to confirm, and nothing you did not ask for.
