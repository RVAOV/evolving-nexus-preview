Privacy Policy

This notice explains what happens to your email address if you join the Evolving Nexus mailing list, and the rights you have over it.

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Who we are

Evolving Nexus (“we”, “us”) is the data controller for the personal data described in this notice.

Controller: Evolving Nexus
Contact: hello@evolvingnexus.com

What we collect

Only your email address, and only when you choose to submit it through the sign-up form on this site.

We do not ask for your name, location, or any other detail. We do not buy, rent or otherwise obtain contact details from third parties.

Why we collect it, and on what basis

We use your email address for one purpose: to send you occasional updates from Evolving Nexus: reflections on consciousness, meaning and reality, and news of forthcoming courses, research, publications and events.

Our lawful basis is your consent, given when you submit the form and confirm your subscription. You can withdraw that consent at any time, and withdrawing it is as easy as giving it.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, no advertising pixels, and no third-party tracking scripts. The fonts are served from our own domain rather than a font network, so loading this page does not disclose your IP address to any third party.

That is why you have not been shown a cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Who else handles your data

We use an email delivery provider to store the mailing list and send messages. Our provider is MailerLite, acting as our processor under a data processing agreement. They may process your data outside the UK or EEA; where they do, the transfer is covered by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

We do not sell, rent or trade your email address, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If the list has been dormant for more than 24 months we will either re-confirm your interest or remove you.

After you unsubscribe we retain a minimal suppression record (your address, and the fact that you opted out) so that we do not accidentally contact you again. This is the one piece of data we keep on the basis of our legitimate interest in honouring your choice.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to how we process it;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise any of these, email hello@evolvingnexus.com. We will respond within one month. There is no charge.

Every email we send also carries a one-click unsubscribe link, which takes effect immediately and requires no explanation.

Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or, if you are in the EEA, to your national data protection authority.

Changes

If we change how we handle your data we will update this page and revise the date above. If the change is significant, we will tell subscribers by email before it takes effect.
